Sourdough Discard Pan & Cast-Iron Pizza Dough

Yield
3 (13×9) pan pizzas or 6 (10-inch) cast-iron pizzas
Cold ferment
12–24 hours

Ingredients

  • 900 gBread flour
  • 560 gWater
  • 200 gSourdough discard (unfed is fine)
  • 30 gOlive oil
  • 18 gSalt
  • 3 gInstant yeast

Method

  1. Combine water, sourdough discard, and yeast in the stand-mixer bowl.
  2. Add flour and salt. Mix on speed 1 for 2–3 minutes, until no dry flour remains.
  3. Add olive oil and mix on speed 2 for 5–6 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  4. Transfer to a lightly oiled container. Rest 1 hour at room temperature. Fold once after 30 minutes; add a second fold near the end of the hour only if the dough still feels slack.
  5. Refrigerate 12–24 hours.
  6. Remove 30–45 minutes before shaping.
  7. Divide into 3 balls of about 570 g for 13×9 pans, or 6 balls of about 280 g for 10-inch cast-iron pizzas.
  8. Form loose balls and rest 30–45 minutes before stretching.
  9. Preheat the oven to 475°F with the empty 13×9 pan or cast-iron skillet inside.
  10. Carefully load the stretched dough into the preheated pan; do not use parchment or proof in the pan. Bake 13×9 pan pizzas 15–18 minutes, or 10-inch cast-iron pizzas 12–14 minutes, until the bottom is crisp and the crust is golden.
  11. Immediately after baking, optionally brush the crust with olive oil mixed with grated garlic and a pinch of salt.

The final frame

What success looks like

  • Crisp bottom with light chew
  • Airy, structured interior
  • Stretches without tearing
  • Balanced discard flavor
