# This Is Bread Pitt

Bread Pitt began as a sourdough starter and slowly expanded into a larger collection of recipes, baking experiments, photos, notes, and bread-related nonsense.

This is technically **Bread Pitt 2.0**.

Bread Pitt 1.0 lived in Florida and did not survive the move to Texas. Somewhere between packing, driving, unpacking, and trying to remember which box contained the important kitchen items, the original starter was lost.

Like any respectable franchise, Bread Pitt was rebooted.

The current starter, **Brad Pitt**, was created in July 2026. His progress has been documented through daily feedings, photographs, rise measurements, smell reports, texture observations, and a running comparison to the career of the other Brad Pitt.

## About the Baker

I am not an expert baker.

I do have some experience, and I have successfully made enough bread to know that I enjoy it. I have also made enough questionable bread to know that confidence and competence are not always the same thing.

I like to learn, experiment, adjust recipes, and figure out why something worked or why it turned into an unusually dense flour-based object.

Bread Pitt will document both.

The successful loaves deserve to be recorded, but so do the failures. A bad result is still useful if I can figure out what caused it. It may also be funny, which is nearly as important.

## What Bread Pitt Includes

Bread Pitt currently includes:

- The ongoing care and development of Brad Pitt
- Feeding, temperature, rise, aroma, and fermentation observations
- Sourdough and yeast-based recipes
- Baking experiments and results
- Equipment notes and practical techniques
- Recipe changes based on actual bakes
- Photos of successful bread
- Photos of bread that had other plans
- Future kitchen temperature and humidity sensor data
- A public Bread Pitt section on BradCooke.com
- The BreadPitt.net domain, because apparently this is serious now

## Project Principles

Bread Pitt should be useful, approachable, and slightly ridiculous.

Recipes should be clear enough to cook from without scrolling through a childhood memory, a vacation story, and twelve paragraphs about the emotional significance of flour.

Recommendations should be based on actual experience whenever possible.

Failures should not be quietly removed from the record.

Experiments should include enough information to understand what changed and whether it helped.

Bread Pitt is also always nut-free.

## Current Status

Brad Pitt is active and getting close to his first full sourdough loaf.

The initial recipe collection has been gathered and is being standardized into a consistent format. Some recipes are well tested. Others are still waiting for their chance to prove themselves or embarrass me.

The project structure, website content, journal policy, and sensor integration are still being developed.

## Where This Is Going

The long-term goal is to connect the starter journal, recipes, baking results, photographs, and environmental data into one useful record.

Bread Pitt may eventually include:

- Starter health summaries
- Automated temperature and humidity reporting
- Fermentation comparisons
- Recipe revision histories
- Equipment and ingredient recommendations
- A searchable recipe collection
- Detailed bake results
- A public timeline of Brad Pitt's rise, fall, and inevitable comeback

This entry marks the beginning of Bread Pitt as a formally initialized Abbey project.

It also marks the beginning of Bread Pitt 2.0.

This time, he is not getting lost in the move.
