# Bread Pitt Starter Records

This directory contains the canonical record of Bread Pitt's sourdough starter,
Brad Pitt.

## Structure

- `profile.md` — stable identity, origin, and current feeding routine
- `records/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — one record for each feeding cycle

The record date is the date the cycle began with a feeding.

A feeding and the observations that follow it belong in the same record. This
keeps rise, aroma, texture, peak behavior, photographs, and final outcome tied
to the feeding that produced them.

## Record Rules

Each record must identify:

- the starter
- the date the cycle began
- whether the record is active or complete
- feeding time and its precision
- ingredient amounts and their precision
- observed rise and peak behavior when known
- observations made during the cycle
- the cycle assessment
- associated media or its import status

Do not create precise values from approximate memories or photographs.

Use these precision values:

- `exact` — directly recorded at the time
- `approximate` — recorded as a range or approximate time
- `established-routine` — reconstructed from the known normal feeding routine
- `unknown` — not documented

## Media

Starter photographs belong under:

`media/starter/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD/`

The directory date must match the feeding-cycle record that the photographs
document.

Original filenames may be preserved in the media directory. Published
derivatives can later receive stable web filenames during the publishing
process.

## Publishing

The Markdown front matter contains structured information for future timelines,
summaries, and analysis. The Markdown body preserves the human-readable
observations and assessment.

The canonical record belongs here even when a shorter or differently formatted
version is later published on the website.
