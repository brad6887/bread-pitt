# July 28 Starter Cycle

## Observations

### Immediately After Feeding

The mixture looked smooth and slightly thicker, with visible whole wheat
flecks. The level represented the normal post-feed baseline.

### About 12 Hours After Feeding

The starter was still around double its starting level.

Small bubbles were distributed evenly through the starter, with a few larger
bubbles visible. The top had flattened and was beginning to wrinkle.

There was no dramatic collapse and the starter had not become runny. It
appeared to be at peak or just past it and may have reached its highest point
an hour or two earlier.

## Assessment

This was another strong and predictable cycle. Brad Pitt approximately doubled,
developed an even bubble structure, and held close to peak for long enough to
be observed around twelve hours after feeding.

The feeding quantities are based on the established routine rather than a
separately recorded measurement for this specific feeding.

The next feeding occurred early at 4:40 AM on July 29 in preparation for the
first Bread Pitt 2.0 loaf.
