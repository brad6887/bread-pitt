# Brad Pitt

Brad Pitt is the active sourdough starter used by the Bread Pitt project.

This is Bread Pitt 2.0. The original starter was lost during the move from
Florida to Texas, and the current starter was created in July 2026.

The current routine uses equal amounts of bread flour and whole wheat flour at
100% hydration. The usual feeding window is between 7:00 and 8:00 AM Central
Time, although individual records override the normal routine when a feeding
happens at a different time.

Bread Pitt documents successful growth, questionable behavior, experiments,
bakes, and failures. The project and every recipe associated with it remain
nut-free.
